The Festival

A new home for world cinema

The International Cinema Festival brings together directors, producers and audiences in one global digital edition. Selected films are presented to an international jury and a worldwide public, with every work given the same care.

Information

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit a film?

Any filmmaker in the world, from independent creators to production companies.

How do I send my film?

Use the submission form with a private Vimeo or YouTube screener link and its password.

Can I enter more than one film?

Yes. Send a separate submission for each film.

Is AI-generated work accepted?

Yes. The AI & Neural Cinema section is dedicated to it.

Which languages are accepted?

Films in any language, with English subtitles for non-English dialogue.
